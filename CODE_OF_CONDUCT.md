# Code of Conduct

HyperHelix is an experimental process framework, but collaboration here should still be practical, respectful, and evidence-oriented.

## Expected Behavior

- Be direct without being hostile.
- Critique artifacts, procedures, and evidence rather than people.
- Prefer reproducible examples, concrete file references, and mechanical checks.
- Treat ambiguity as a design signal to clarify, not as someone else's fault.
- Keep private information, credentials, and sensitive reports out of public discussion.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information or secrets.
- Deliberately bypassing project safety rules.
- Misrepresenting validation results or human approvals.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments and contributions that violate this code. Repeated or severe violations may lead to blocking from the project.
